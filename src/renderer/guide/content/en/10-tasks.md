# Tasks and maintenance

The **Tasks** screen holds the hotel's jobs: today's, late ones and upcoming ones. Repeating jobs (calling rooms that have not checked out at 12:00 every day, the air-conditioning filter every month) become tasks by themselves. The **Maintenance** screen follows periodic maintenance and deep cleaning.

## The task list {#task-list}

Open **Daily work → Tasks** in the left menu.

![The Tasks screen](tasks)

- The buttons at the top choose the list: **Today**, **Late**, **Open**, **Upcoming**, **Done**.
- The **Assigned to** box: **All tasks** or **Assigned to me**. Use the **Room** box to see one room's tasks.
- A late task shows the red **Late** label.
- The "Legal", "Maintenance" and "Automatic" labels tell where a task came from.

## Marking a task done {#complete-task}

Tick the box at the start of the task. "Task marked as done" appears; under the task you see who did it and when. If you ticked it by mistake, click the box again to undo.

If a task is no longer needed, choose **More actions → Cancel task** and write the reason. The task is not deleted; it stays as cancelled.

## New task {#new-task}

1. Press **New task**.
2. Type a **Title** (for example "Clean the air-conditioning filter in 302") and, if you like, a **Description**.
3. **Assign to**: a **Person**, a **Role** (for example Housekeeping) or a **Shift** (for example Night).
4. **Room (optional)**: choose it if the task is about a room.
5. **Repeat**: **Does not repeat**, **Every day at a set time**, **On chosen weekdays**, **On a day of the month** or **Every N stays (per room)**.
6. Choose the date and **Time** and save.

With "Every N stays" the count goes up with each check-out from that room; for example a deep clean every 10 stays.

## Suggested tasks and legal reminders {#suggestions}

On the Tasks screen, the **Suggested tasks** box comes with the first setup (for example "Call rooms that have not checked out", "Check the floor storerooms (towels, sheets, supplies)", "Enter the clean slip"). Add the useful ones with **Add to tasks** and say **Not needed** to the others.

**Legal reminders** come ready when the hotel's country is Türkiye: the Ministry of Tourism accommodation statistics (1st–10th of each month) and checking the KBS check-out reports. Switch off the ones you do not need. For periodic checks such as the fire extinguisher or the lift, add a reminder on the **Maintenance** screen.

All regular jobs are in **Settings → Task suggestions**; add your own regular task there with **Add task** (every day, every week, every month, every few months, every year). When you switch off a suggestion, its tasks not yet done are cancelled.

## Maintenance {#maintenance}

Open **Operations → Maintenance** in the left menu. Here are the **recurring maintenance jobs** such as cleaning the water tank, the air-conditioning filter or refilling the fire extinguishers. Maintenance that is coming up appears in Tasks a week before.

![The Maintenance screen](maintenance)

To add a reminder:

1. Press **Add a reminder**.
2. **What needs doing?** (for example "Air-conditioning filters").
3. **Every** and **Unit**: for example 6 + **months**.
4. **First due** and **Remind how many days before?**
5. **Company / tradesperson (phone book)**: if the company doing the work is in the contacts, choose it; if you do it yourselves, **We do it ourselves**.
6. Choose **Responsible (optional)** and save.

When the job is done, press **Done** on the line, enter **Done on** and a note if you like (company, receipt no.). The next date is worked out by itself. You can also add ready suggestions from the **Example maintenance reminders** box.

## Deep clean counter {#deep-clean}

Each room is deep cleaned every so many stays (for example every 10 stays). The **Deep clean counter** shows how many stays each room has had; when the count is reached, a job appears in Tasks. After the deep clean, press **Cleaned** for that room in the counter on the Maintenance screen; the counter starts again.

How many stays between deep cleans is changed in **Settings → Task suggestions**.
