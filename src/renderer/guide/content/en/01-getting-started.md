# Getting started

This section is for everyone opening the program for the first time: signing in, locking the screen, changing the language, finding your way around the menu and getting help when you are stuck.

## What is Bismuth? {#what-is-bismuth}

Bismuth keeps all the hotel's daily work in one place: reservations, payments, the cash drawer, expenses, guests, room cleaning, issues, tasks, shift handovers and staff. Information you used to keep on separate Excel sheets is connected here: when you record a payment, the colour in the calendar, the line in the ledger and the money in the cash drawer all change together.

- The program works **without the internet** too. The internet is only used for optional information such as exchange rates, the weather and holiday dates.
- Your data stays on the hotel's own computer and is not sent anywhere.
- No record is ever deleted. A wrong record is **cancelled**; who cancelled it, when and why is kept.

## First setup {#setup}

The first time the program opens, the **setup wizard** appears. The hotel owner does this once; it takes about 10–15 minutes. The steps, in order:

1. **Language and theme**: the program's language and a light or dark look.
2. **How it runs**: will this computer work on its own, be the hotel's main computer, or connect to another Bismuth?
3. **Hotel**: the hotel's name, country, city, time zone, check-in and check-out times.
4. **Currencies**: the base currency all totals are counted in (usually Turkish lira) and the foreign currencies you accept.
5. **Room types**: the types in your hotel and their suggested nightly rates.
6. **Rooms**: you create the rooms quickly from the number of floors and rooms per floor, then correct numbers, types and beds one by one.
7. **Payments and sales**: payment methods (cash, card, IBAN…), sales channels (direct, Booking, Airbnb…) and extra services.
8. **Staff and shifts**: day-off and pay rules, shift times, task suggestions.
9. **Employees**: if you like, add employees and open user accounts for them.
10. **Calendar**: days to highlight (for example the weekend), holidays and closing the day.
11. **Owner account**: the first account, which sees everything; your name, user name, password and an optional PIN.
12. **Summary**: check the details and press **Finish setup**.

![Setup wizard](setup)

> [!warning]
> When setup is finished, the **recovery codes** are shown once. Write them on paper or copy them and keep them somewhere safe. If you forget your password, you set a new one with one of these codes. You cannot go on until you tick **I have saved the codes**.

Almost everything you enter in the wizard can be changed later in **Settings**. Only the base currency cannot be changed once a reservation or payment has been recorded.

## Signing in {#sign-in}

Everyone signs in **under their own name**. That way it is always clear who wrote what, who received the money and who read the handover.

1. On the sign-in screen, click **your name** in the list.
2. Choose **PIN** or **Password**. If you have a PIN, the program asks for it first.
3. Type your PIN or password and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click **Sign in**.

![Sign-in screen](login)

- If your name is not in the list, press **Sign in with a username** and type your user name and password.
- If an account was just opened for you, the first sign-in shows **Choose a new password**: type the temporary password you were given and your new password twice, then press **Save password**.
- After five wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes. This is a safety measure; wait, or reset your password.

## Forgot your password {#forgot-password}

1. On the sign-in screen press **Forgot password**.
2. Type your user name and one of your recovery codes.
3. Type your new password twice and press **Reset password**.

Each recovery code works once. If you have no code, the hotel owner can reset your password in **Settings → Users and roles** and give you a new temporary one.

## The account menu {#account-menu}

The button with your name in the top right corner opens the **account menu**. From there:

- **Change my password**: type your current password and the new one twice.
- **Set a PIN** or **Change PIN**: a 4–8 digit PIN lets you pick your name from the list and sign in or unlock quickly. Easy PINs such as 1111 or 1234 are not accepted.
- **Renew recovery codes**: you get new codes; unused old codes stop working.
- **Lock** and **Sign out**.

## Locking the screen {#lock}

Lock the screen when you leave the desk, so nobody can write records under your name.

- Choose **Lock** in the account menu, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> on a Mac).
- To unlock, type your PIN or password and press **Unlock**. You return to the screen you were on.
- If someone else is taking over, they choose **Sign in as someone else** on the lock screen.
- If the mouse and keyboard are not used for a while, the window locks by itself. The hotel owner sets this time in **Settings → General → Screen lock**.

> [!tip]
> Use **Sign out** at the end of your shift. Locking is for stepping away for a short time; signing out hands the computer to the next person.

## Language, theme and look {#language}

The program works in Turkish, English and Russian. Each user chooses their own language; one person's choice does not affect anyone else.

1. Open **Settings** in the left menu; the **General** section opens.
2. Choose your language in the **Language** box. Menus and buttons change at once.
3. The same section also has **Theme** (Light, Dark, Same as the computer), **Density** (Comfortable, Compact), **Show decimals** and **Start screen** (Calendar or Today).

![Settings, General section](settings-general)

## Menu and screens {#menu}

On a computer, the **sidebar** on the left shows every screen in groups: Daily work, Guests, Money, Operations, Staff, Reports and System. Use the button at the top left to fold the menu in and out. The menu only shows the screens your role may see.

The top bar, from left to right: the hotel's name and today's date, the **Search or go to** box, the **?** (guide) button, the cash drawer and your account menu.

- **Quick search**: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) or the **Search or go to** box at the top. Type the name of a screen and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>; you can change the language and theme here too. To look up a number in the contacts, type "contacts" first.
- In windows, <kbd>Enter</kbd> saves and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes.

## Keyboard shortcuts {#keyboard}

You do not need the mouse for everyday tasks. When you are not typing in a field, press:

- <kbd>N</kbd>: the **New reservation** window opens, and the calendar with it, whatever screen you are on. If a cell is selected in the calendar, the reservation starts for that room and day.
- <kbd>P</kbd>: the add-payment window opens. If a reservation's panel is open, the payment goes to that reservation; otherwise it opens on the **Ledger and cash** screen as a payment of that day.
- <kbd>T</kbd>: the calendar goes back to today and today's cell is selected, so you can go on with the arrow keys.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>/</kbd>: quick search.
- <kbd>?</kbd> or <kbd>F1</kbd>: the list of all shortcuts. You can also open it from the quick search by typing "shortcuts".

## On the phone {#on-the-phone}

On the phone the menu is at the bottom of the screen: **Today · Calendar · Guests · Cash · More**. The other screens are under **More**. Forms open from the bottom; buttons are big enough to press comfortably with a finger. To connect from a phone, see [Remote access](guide:remote).

## This guide {#guide}

- The **?** button in the top bar of every screen opens the section about that screen.
- The whole guide is in **Settings → Guide**; the left menu also has **Guide** in the **System** group.
- Use the **Who it is for** box to see only the sections you need: Reception, Housekeeping, Accounting, Owner and manager.
- Type a word in the search box (for example "deposit"); the places it appears are listed.
- **Print section** prints the open section.
- You can watch the short **tour** shown at your first sign-in again with **Start the tour**.
