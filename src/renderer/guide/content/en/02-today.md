# The Today screen

The Today screen is the summary of the day: who is arriving, who is leaving, which room is dirty, who is missing from KBS, which task is waiting. Look at this screen first when your shift starts.

## Opening the screen {#open-today}

Click **Today** in the **Daily work** group of the left menu. On the phone it is the first button of the bottom menu. If you set your start screen to **Today** in Settings, the program starts here every time.

![The Today screen](today)

## The numbers at the top {#today-numbers}

There are five boxes at the top of the screen:

- **Occupancy tonight**: how many rooms are occupied tonight (for example 14/19 rooms).
- **Arriving today**: rooms checking in today; underneath, how many have already checked in.
- **Leaving today**: rooms checking out today; underneath, how many have already left.
- **Cash in the drawer**: cash the owner or manager has not received yet.
- **KBS missing**: people staying in the hotel who are not reported to KBS. It is red when above zero.

## Unread shift handover {#today-handover}

If the previous shift wrote a handover and you have not read it yet, an **Unread shift handover** box appears at the very top. Press **Read the handover**; the shift handover screen opens. Do not forget to press **I've read it** once you have read it. Details: [Shift handover and notes](guide:shift).

## Leaving today {#today-departures}

The heading shows the hotel's check-out time (for example "check-out 12:00"). Each line shows the room number, the guest's name and, in red, any unpaid **Balance**.

- If the guest is still in the room, the end of the line says **In the room**.
- If check-out time has passed and the guest has not left, the line is framed in yellow as **Past check-out time**. Call these rooms.
- Once checked out, the line shows **Checked out** and the time.
- You check guests out in the calendar: open the reservation and press **Check out**. See [Checking out](guide:reservations#check-out).

## Arriving today {#today-arrivals}

Guests checking in today. If they have checked in, the line shows **Checked in** and the time; if not, the number of nights and the reservation note (for example "arriving late"). When the guest arrives, open the reservation in the calendar and press **Check in**. See [Checking in](guide:reservations#check-in).

## The other boxes {#today-cards}

Most boxes have an **All** button at the top right that opens that job's screen.

- **Today's tasks**: jobs due today and late ones. Ticking the box marks the task done; who did it and when is recorded.
- **KBS gaps**: people not reported to KBS, room by room. After entering them in KBS, mark them on the [In-house guests](guide:in-house) screen.
- **Dirty rooms**: rooms checked out and not cleaned yet.
- **Open issues**: issues not fixed yet; those reported today are marked.
- **Weather**: today and this week for the hotel's city (when there is internet).
- **Frequently called numbers**: numbers you made favourites in the contacts; click to copy, tap on the phone to call.
- **Today's special days**: holidays, fairs and notes you added to the calendar.
- If you have permission, the staff panel: who is off today, how much advance each person has.
- **My working hours**: employees who cannot see the staff screen enter their start and end times here with **Enter today's hours**.

> [!tip]
> Between the 1st and the 10th of each month, a **Ministry of Tourism accommodation statistics** reminder appears. Press **Open Tourism statistics** and prepare the statistics. See [Ministry statistics](guide:tourism).

> [!warning]
> If you see a **Backup warning** box, backups are not being made. Press **Open backup settings** or tell the hotel owner. See [Backup](guide:backup).

## If a box does not load {#today-errors}

If a box says "This part could not be loaded", open the screen again a little later. The other boxes keep working. If you never see a box, your role has no permission for that information.
