# For housekeeping

This section is for housekeepers. Your work in Bismuth is short: see which rooms to clean, mark the room you cleaned, report issues, mark the tasks given to you as done and tell reception about items you find.

## Signing in {#hk-sign-in}

1. Open Bismuth on the computer or the phone.
2. Click your name in the list, type your PIN and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
3. When you finish, press your name at the top right and choose **Sign out**.

The hotel owner will show you how to connect from the phone; see [Remote access](guide:remote).

## Which rooms today? {#hk-today}

After you sign in, the **Today** screen opens. The **Dirty rooms** box shows the rooms to clean; **Today's tasks** shows the jobs given to you. A room that has checked out becomes dirty by itself and appears on this list.

On the **Room status** screen the rooms are shown floor by floor: which is dirty, which has a guest, which has a check-in today. Clean the dirty rooms with a check-in today first.

![Room status](rooms)

## Marking a room you cleaned {#hk-mark-clean}

1. Open the **Room status** screen.
2. Press the **Cleaned** button in the box of the room you cleaned.

The room becomes **Clean**; reception sees it at once. If something is missing in the room, click the room, type it in the **Note** box (for example "sheet missing") and press **Save note**.

> [!tip]
> You can keep working with the WhatsApp group: if you write "302+", reception marks the room clean in Bismuth. If you mark it yourself in Bismuth, reception has less to do.

## Reporting an issue {#hk-issue}

If you see something broken in a room:

1. Open the **Issues** screen and press **Report an issue**.
2. Choose the room and type a short title (for example "Shower head broken").
3. Choose the **Severity**; if it is urgent, **Urgent**.
4. Take a photo with the phone and add it.
5. Save.

Reception and the owner see the issue at once. Details: [Reporting an issue](guide:rooms#issues).

## Tasks {#hk-tasks}

The **Tasks** screen shows the jobs given to you or your role (for example "check the floor storerooms"). When you finish, tick the box next to the task. Who did it and when is recorded by itself.

## Lost and found {#hk-lost-items}

If you find an item left in a room, give it to reception and tell them; reception records it on the **Lost and found** screen. Your name is written as the person who found it.

## Your working hours {#hk-hours}

On the Today screen, press **Enter today's hours** in the **My working hours** box; type your start and end time and save. The manager approves overtime.
