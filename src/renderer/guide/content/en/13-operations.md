# Contacts, supplies, laundry, lost and found

This section covers the hotel's operations screens: numbers you call often (**Contacts**), product purchases and prices (**Supplies**), laundry deliveries (**Laundry**) and items found in the hotel (**Lost and found**).

## Contacts {#contacts}

Open **Operations → Contacts** in the left menu. Suppliers, the laundry, plumbers, electricians, taxis and emergency numbers are kept here.

![Contacts](contacts)

- **Click a number to copy it**; on the phone, tap it to call.
- Use the category buttons at the top (Supplier, Laundry, Plumber…) to narrow the list; type a name, note or number in the search box.
- Contacts you star as **Favourite** stay at the top of the list and in the **Frequently called numbers** box on the Today screen.

To add a contact press **Add contact**: **Person / company**, **Category** (if it is not in the list, **Your own category…**), **Contact person**, **Phone numbers** (several numbers with labels: mobile, work…), **Email**, **Address**, **Note** (for example "Delivers Tuesday and Friday").

To bring in your contact list from Excel all at once, press **Import**; paste the lines or open a CSV file. Each line: name; phone; category.

In quick search (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>), type "contacts" and then a name to get the number straight away.

## Supplies {#supply}

The **Operations → Supplies** screen follows the products you buy, their last purchase prices and price rises. There are three tabs: **Products**, **Purchases**, **Monthly summary**.

![Supplies](supply)

Add the products first: **Add product** → **Product name** (for example "Spring water 0.5 L"), **Unit** (pcs, kg, l, pack, bag), **Category**, supplier (from the contacts). No price is typed here; it comes from purchases.

When a delivery slip arrives:

1. Press **Enter a purchase**.
2. Choose the supplier, **Paid by** and the **Expense section**.
3. Under **Products**, type the product, quantity and unit price on each line; add lines with **Add a product**. Add a product that is not in the list with **Add a product not on the list**.
4. If the slip's total is different (delivery, deposit), correct the **Expense total**.
5. If the money came out of the drawer, tick **Paid from the cash drawer**; add a photo of the slip if you like.
6. Save.

The purchase is written into the ledger **once, as one expense line**; the products' price history is updated. If the last price is higher than the one before, the product is marked as a **price rise** (for example "+12%"). Click a product to see its price history as a chart and a table.

## Laundry {#laundry}

The **Operations → Laundry** screen counts the dirty laundry going out and the clean laundry coming back, item by item; it shows missing and stained pieces; at the end of the month it compares the invoice with the slips.

![Laundry](laundry)

First add the laundry to **Contacts** with the **Laundry** category, then choose it on the screen. The laundry items (sheet, duvet cover, towel…) are in **Settings → Lists → Laundry items**.

- When dirty laundry goes out, press **Hand over dirty**; type the number of each item and save.
- When clean laundry comes back, press **Enter clean slip**; in the slip's order, type the number of each item and the number **Stained**. <kbd>Enter</kbd> moves to the next box and saves on the last one. Add the **Slip no.** and a photo of the slip if you like.

The item table shows the pieces **Sent**, **Back**, **In the wash** and **Missing**. Pieces not back from an earlier delivery count as **missing**; more pieces coming back than were sent are flagged separately.

At the end of the month:

1. In the **Unit prices** card, enter the agreed prices with **Enter price** (when prices go up, enter the new price with the date it applies from; old slips keep the old price).
2. **Month's account** shows the amount worked out from the slips. When the laundry's invoice arrives, type it with **Enter invoice**; the program tells you if there is a difference.
3. When you pay the invoice, press **Mark as paid**; the invoice becomes one expense line in the ledger on the last day of the month.

A wrong delivery record is cancelled with **Cancel** in the **Delivery log**.

## Lost and found {#lost-items}

Items guests leave behind are kept on the **Guests → Lost and found** screen.

![Lost and found](lost-items)

1. Press **Log a found item**.
2. **Item** (for example "black charger"), **Found on**, **Found by**.
3. **Where it was found**: **In a room** (choose the room) or **In a common area** (type the place).
4. **Kept at** (for example "reception, drawer 2").
5. If you like, the **Likely owner**, a photo and a note; save.

When the item is returned, press **Return**; type **Returned to** and the day. If nobody claims it for a long time, close it with **Dispose of**, writing the reason. The record is not deleted; if you changed it by mistake, make it "Kept" again with **Undo**. The filter at the top separates **Kept**, **Returned** and **Disposed of** items.
