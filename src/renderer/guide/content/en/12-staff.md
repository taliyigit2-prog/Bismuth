# Staff and salaries

The **Staff** screen holds the employee cards, the monthly salary table, advances, days off, working time and the 14-day schedule. Salary and advance payments are written into the ledger as expenses by themselves. The owner and accounting see this screen; the manager handles days off and overtime.

Open **Staff → Staff** in the left menu. There are four tabs: **Employees**, **Salary table**, **Days off and schedule**, **Work time**.

![The Staff screen](staff)

The boxes at the top: the number of employees and how many are at work today, **Off today**, this month's **Advances**, **Salary due**, **Days off used this month**.

## New employee {#new-employee}

1. Press **New employee**.
2. Type the **First name**, **Last name**, **Position** (for example reception), **Phone**, **Start date** and **Monthly salary**.
3. If the employee has an account for the program, link it in the **User account** box. Accounts are opened in **Settings → Users and roles**.
4. Save.

Click an employee to open their card: this month's summary, records, salary history and buttons.

## Changing a salary {#salary-change}

On the card press **Change salary**; type the new amount and the **Valid from** date. Months from that date on are counted with the new salary; earlier months do not change. Old salaries stay in the **Salary history**.

## Giving an advance {#advance}

1. On the card or in the salary table press **Give advance**.
2. Type the **Advance amount**; the salary left for this month is shown above.
3. If the money came out of the reception drawer, tick **Paid from the drawer**; it is taken off the cash in the drawer.
4. Save.

The advance is written into the ledger as an expense and deducted from this month's salary. Whether advances may be paid from the drawer is a setting (**Settings → Staff rules**).

## The salary table and paying salaries {#salary-table}

The **Salary table** tab shows one line per employee for the chosen month: **Salary**, **Advances**, **Unused day-off pay**, **Overtime**, **Bonus**, **Deductions**, **Net due**, **Paid**, **Remaining**. Use the arrows to move to the previous or next month.

To pay a salary:

1. Press **Pay salary** on the line.
2. Enter the **Amount paid** and choose the **Payment method**. A salary can also be paid in parts.
3. Press **Record payment**.

The payment is written into the ledger as a salary expense. For a bonus or a deduction (for example "broken glass") use **Bonus or deduction**; it changes the net amount without writing an expense into the ledger.

When the month is fully paid, lock it with **Lock the month**. In a locked month salary, advance and day-off records cannot be changed; if needed, someone with permission opens it with **Unlock**, giving a reason.

> [!tip]
> A wrong advance or payment is voided from **This month's records** on the card; its expense in the ledger is voided with it. Nothing is deleted.

## Days off {#leave}

1. On the **Days off and schedule** tab press **Record a day off**.
2. Choose the employee and the **Type**: **Day off** (taken from the monthly allowance), **Annual leave**, **Sick leave**, **Unpaid** or **Worked on a holiday**.
3. Enter the first day (**From**) and the number of **Days**; underneath you see how many days are used this month and how many will be left.
4. Save.

The **14-day schedule** shows everyone's next two weeks in colours: Day shift, Night, Day off, Annual leave, Sick. The monthly day-off allowance, whether unused days are paid and whether they carry over are in **Settings → Staff rules**.

## Working time and overtime {#overtime}

Employees enter their own start and end times (in the **My working hours** box on the Today screen). On the **Work time** tab the manager or owner:

- sees and, if needed, corrects each employee's **Start–end** times and the time **Worked**, day by day;
- sees time beyond the daily working hours as **Suggested overtime** and approves it with **approve**;
- marks a day as a **Holiday**.

Overtime pay follows the hotel's rule (not paid, a fixed hourly amount, or a multiple of the hourly wage).

## Pay rules {#pay-rules}

The hotel's general rules are in **Settings → Staff rules**: monthly days off, whether unused days are paid and at what multiplier, how the daily wage is worked out (divided by the days of the month or by a fixed number), daily working hours, overtime pay, the holiday work multiplier, bonuses and advances from the drawer.

If one employee needs a rule of their own, open **Pay rules** on their card; a rule you switch on there applies only to that employee.

## An employee who leaves {#leaving}

On the card choose **Mark as left the job**, enter the **Last working day** and press **Mark as left the job** in the window. The card is not deleted; past salary and day-off records are kept. The last month's salary is counted from the days worked. To see former employees in the list, use **Show former employees**.
