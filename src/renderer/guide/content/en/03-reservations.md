# How to write a reservation

Reservations are written and followed on the **Calendar** screen. This section explains, step by step, how to read the calendar, write a new reservation, check in and out, make changes, use the park area, close rooms and cancel.

## Reading the calendar {#calendar}

Open **Daily work → Calendar** in the left menu. In most hotels this is the first screen when the program opens.

![The calendar](calendar)

- Each **row** is a room, each **column** a day. Today's column is marked with a coloured line; highlighted days (for example the weekend) have a different colour.
- Each reservation is a **bar** from check-in to check-out. The check-out day is not a night: a reservation from 3 to 5 March is 2 nights.
- The bar's colour shows **payment night by night**. Default colours: **Paid** red, **Unpaid** yellow, **Partly paid / deposit** orange, **Day use** blue. The **legend** under the calendar explains the colours.
- Small icons on a bar: note, guest request, VIP (crown), **KBS missing** (shield), deposit, child.
- The coloured dot next to the room number is the room's cleaning status; a wrench icon means there is an open issue.
- A striped area with a lock or wrench icon means the room is **closed for sale** on those days.

Moving around:

- The **Today** button goes back to today; the arrows move a week forward or back.
- Use the **Month** and **Year** boxes to jump to a month; **Go to date** picks a day. The calendar reaches back to the year of the oldest stay (or to **Settings → Calendar → Calendar start year**) and forward to ten years from today.
- Clicking a day heading opens that day's holidays and notes; you can write a note there with **Add note**.
- With the keyboard: arrow keys move between cells, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd> jump a week, <kbd>Home</kbd> goes to today, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the selected cell, <kbd>N</kbd> starts a new reservation.

## New reservation {#new-reservation}

1. In the calendar, click the **empty cell** of the guest's room on the check-in day. The room and date fill in by themselves. Or press the **New reservation** button at the top right.
2. Type the guest's name in the **Guest** box. If they stayed before, they appear in the list; pick them. For a new guest, choose the **New guest: …** line and fill in **First name**, **Last name** and **Phone** (optional).
3. Check the **Check-in** and **Check-out** dates. If both are the same day, the reservation is saved as **day use**.
4. Choose the room in the **Rooms** list. If a group has several rooms, tick them all. A room that is booked on those dates is marked "taken".
5. Choose the **Sales channel** (Direct, Booking, Airbnb…). If the channel gave a booking number, type it in **Booking no. in the channel**.
6. **Status**: **Confirmed** if it is settled, **Tentative** if the guest has not confirmed yet.
7. Price: type the price in **Nightly rate (all nights)**. Underneath, the program shows the room type's suggested price and the weekend or special day price; if the guest stayed before, what the guest **last paid** too. If nights have different prices, open **Rate by night** and type each night separately.
8. If you like, add **Tags** (Group, VIP…) and a **Note** (for example "arriving late, baby cot").
9. Check the number of nights and the total in the summary at the bottom and press **Save**.

![New reservation window](new-reservation)

The reservation appears in the calendar at once; the guest card is created for you.

> [!warning]
> If the guest is **on the blacklist**, a red warning appears in the window. It cannot be saved until you read the warning and tick **I read the warning, save anyway**. A reservation for someone marked "Block" can only be saved by the owner or manager.

> [!tip]
> If the guest has an **important standing note** (for example "wants a high floor"), you are asked to tick **I have read this note** before saving. Notes at the information level appear in a yellow box.

## Opening a reservation {#reservation-panel}

Click a bar in the calendar; the reservation **panel** opens at the side (from the bottom on the phone). At the top are the guest's name, the room, the dates and these buttons: **Check in**, **Check out**, **Add payment** and **More actions** (three dots).

![Reservation panel](reservation-panel)

The panel has four tabs:

- **Summary**: number of people, balance, contact details, the guest's earlier stays, notes and the guest note.
- **Nights**: each night's price and payment status; changing dates and rooms.
- **Money**: total charges, paid, balance and the list of payments. See [What to do when you take a payment](guide:payments).
- **Occupants**: everyone staying in the room and their KBS status.

To find a guest's reservation, type a name, phone number or reservation code in **Find guest** above the calendar. Type a room number (302, for example) to get that room's stays nearest to today first.

## Room occupants {#occupants}

**Each person** staying in the room has their own line; the first is the person who booked. So that the night shift knows who is who, you can write a **descriptor note** for each person (for example "red hair, glasses").

1. Open the **Occupants** tab in the panel.
2. Press **Add person**; type the **Full name** and, if you like, a **Descriptor note**.
3. If you have entered the person in KBS, tick **Reported to KBS**; the time and your name are recorded.
4. Save with **Add person**.

Bismuth never asks for or keeps ID numbers; identity details are only entered in KBS. For all KBS marks see [In-house guests and KBS](guide:in-house).

## Checking in {#check-in}

When the guest arrives at the hotel:

1. Open the reservation in the calendar.
2. Press **Check in**. With several rooms there is a separate button for each room ("Room 102: check in").
3. On the **Occupants** tab, add everyone staying in the room and mark them as you enter them in KBS.

You cannot check in before the check-in date. If you see the red **Someone is not reported to KBS** warning in the panel, add the people and complete the KBS marks.

## Checking out {#check-out}

1. Open the reservation; if there is a balance on the **Money** tab, take the payment first.
2. Press **Check out**.
3. In the window that opens, mark the people whose KBS check-out you have done as **KBS check-out done**.
4. Confirm with **Check out**.

After check-out the room becomes **Dirty** by itself and goes onto the housekeeping list.

If the guest leaves early, the check-out window says how many nights will be freed ("2 nights will be freed"); those nights and their charges are cancelled and the room can be sold again. If the guest leaves on the check-in day, the first night stays as a **day use**; you can change its price in the **Day use price** field.

## Changing a reservation {#change-reservation}

Open the **Nights** tab in the panel:

- **Change dates**: choose the new check-in and check-out dates and save. A stay that has checked in keeps its check-in date; you can only make the check-out later or earlier.
- **Move to another room**: choose the new room and press **Move**. If the room is booked on those dates, the program tells you.
- **Add room**: adds a second room to the same reservation.
- In the night-by-night price table, change a night's price and press **Save rates**.

Notes are on the **Summary** tab: write them with **Add note** (for example "asked for an airport transfer").

## The park area {#park-area}

Reservations that must leave the calendar but are not cancelled wait in the **park area**: the guest did not come but paid, a refund is pending, or the dates were postponed. When a reservation is parked, its nights are freed and the room can be sold; payments stay on record.

1. Open the reservation; choose **More actions → Park**.
2. Choose the **Reason for parking**: **No-show, paid**, **Refund pending**, **Postponed** or **Other**. Write a note if you like.
3. Confirm with **Park**.

Parked reservations are listed in the **Parked** box under the calendar. If the guest comes back for new dates, press **Restore to calendar** and choose the room, dates and nightly price. Payments stay as they are. If money is to be returned, record the refund with **Enter refund**.

## No-show {#no-show}

If the guest never came, open the reservation, choose **More actions → Mark as no-show** and confirm. The nights are freed and payments stay on record; the reservation is not deleted but kept as "No-show".

- If money is to be returned or the guest will come on another date, first **Park** it (reason: **No-show, paid**). In the park area you can enter the refund with **Enter refund** or move it to new dates with **Restore to calendar**.
- A parked reservation can also be closed later with **Parked reservation actions → Mark as no-show**.

## Cancelling a reservation {#cancel}

1. Open the reservation; choose **More actions → Cancel reservation**.
2. Type the **Reason for cancelling** (required).
3. If **money was collected**, the window shows the amount ("This reservation has ₺1,500 collected") and asks what happens to it:
   - **Enter a refund**: choose the amount and the method; the refund is written to the ledger day and the cash drawer together with the cancellation. It cannot exceed what was collected.
   - **Park as refund pending**: shown only to people who may not refund. The reservation is not cancelled; it waits in the park area under the calendar as "Refund pending" until someone with the right enters the refund and cancels it.
   - **Keep the payments**: for example a cancellation fee; the reason is required and kept on record.
4. Confirm with **Cancel reservation**.

The nights are freed; payments stay on record. The reservation is not deleted; it is kept as "Cancelled". If the guest is in the hotel (checked in), use **Gave up and refund** instead of cancelling (below).

## The guest checked in and gave up: give up and refund {#give-up}

When a guest gave up after entering the room, choose **More actions → Gave up and refund**. There are three ways:

- **Check out the same day**: the guest leaves on the check-in day. The first night stays as a "same-day stay"; fix its price in the check-out window. When the check-out is saved the refund window opens and you refund the surplus.
- **Take the check-in back and cancel**: the stay counts as if it never happened. Only on the check-in day, and it needs the **Taking a check-in back** right (default: owner and manager; the owner can allow reception in Settings → Roles). Type the reason and choose one of the three money options above. If someone in the room is reported to KBS the program warns you; the report stays on record and you make the KBS check-out on the KBS side.
- **Check out early**: the nights left are freed, the nights stayed remain.

A reservation that is closed (checked out, cancelled or no-show) keeps its **More actions** menu too: **Enter a refund** (when money was collected), **History** and **Add note**. Reception can enter refunds; the owner can switch this right off in Settings → Roles.

If the reservation has several rooms, you can cancel just one of them: choose **More actions → Room 102: cancel** and confirm with **Cancel the room**. That room's nights are freed; the other rooms and the payments stay on record.

## Closing a room for sale (block) {#room-block}

If a room will not be sold on certain days (the owner's guest, painting, renovation):

1. Press **Close a room** above the calendar.
2. Choose the **Room**, the **First day** and the **Last day**.
3. **Type**: **Not for sale** (the room is fine but will not be sold) or **Out of order** (the room cannot be used).
4. Type a **Reason** (for example "Owner's guest") and press **Close room**.

Closed days are striped in the calendar. To lift the block, click the striped area and press **Open for sale**. A block that comes from an issue or from the room status lifts by itself when that record is closed.

## Finding a free room {#free-rooms}

When someone on the phone asks "is there a free room on these dates?":

1. Press **Find free rooms** above the calendar.
2. Choose the **Check-in** and **Check-out** dates and, if you like, the **Room type**.
3. Press **Find rooms**. The rooms free for the whole stay are marked in the calendar.

Remove the marks with **Clear marks**.

## Calendar notes and notes for many days {#calendar-notes-quick}

**Note for many days** above the calendar adds the same note to many days (for example "breakfast room closed" every Monday). For holidays and special days: [Exchange rates, weather, special days](guide:currency#special-days).

## The calendar on the phone {#calendar-phone}

On the phone the calendar is a **day list**: **Arrivals**, **Departures**, **Staying** and the number of free rooms. Change the day with the arrows. Tap a line to open the reservation panel from the bottom.
