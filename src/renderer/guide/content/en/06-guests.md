# Guests, companies, blacklist

Every guest has a **card**: contact details, earlier stays, the prices they paid, standing notes and their company. The card is created by itself with the first reservation; you never have to type the same guest again.

## Finding a guest {#find-guest}

Open **Guests → Guests** in the left menu (**Guests** in the phone's bottom menu).

![The Guests screen](guests)

1. Type a first name, last name, phone or company in the search box. Turkish letters do not need to match exactly.
2. Narrow the list with **Filter**: **Returning**, **VIP**, **With standing note**, **With company**, **Possible duplicate**, **On the blacklist**.
3. **Sort**: **Last stay**, **By name**, **Number of stays**, **Total paid**.
4. Click a guest to open the card.

Icons on the lines: a crown marks a VIP guest, a note icon a standing note, a warning icon a possible duplicate, a red icon the blacklist.

To answer "who stayed in 302 in March?", press **Room and dates**: type the room number and pick the first and last day. Guests who stayed in that room on those days are listed with their stays; cancelled and no-show reservations appear too, marked as such. You can also pick only a room or only the days.

## The guest card {#guest-card}

At the top of the card are the guest's name, phone (copied with the button next to it), first visit date and summary boxes: **Last stay**, **Last price paid**, **Average per night**, **Total paid**.

![Guest card](guest-card)

- The **History** tab: upcoming reservations and past stays; each with room, dates, nightly price and balance.
- The **Details** tab: phone, email, language spoken, nationality, notes. Only people with permission see identity details.
- The **Company** tab: the company the guest belongs to and its billing details.
- The **New reservation** button opens the reservation window for this guest. **Use this price for a new reservation** brings the last price paid as a suggestion.

To change details press **Edit**. If you tick **VIP guest**, a crown icon appears in the list and the calendar.

## Tags {#guest-tags}

Use tags to sort guests into your own groups ("Regular", "Company guest", "Wants a quiet room" and so on).

1. Add the tags once in **Settings → Lists → Guest tags**. Switch off a tag you no longer use instead of deleting it.
2. On the guest card, press **Add tag** under the name (**Edit tags** when the guest has some) and tick the boxes. Each tick is saved at once.
3. In the **Guests** list the tags show under the name; narrow the list to one tag with **All tags** next to the search box.

The guest summary in the reservation panel shows the tags too. A switched-off tag stays on the cards, faded and marked "(switched off)", and cannot be given to anyone new. When two cards are merged, the tags move to the card that stays.

## New guest {#new-guest}

Guests are usually added while writing a reservation. To add one without a reservation, press **New guest**; only the **First name** is required. If the program finds a card with the same name or phone, it says "This person may already have a card"; use the existing card with **Open card**.

## Standing note {#standing-note}

If the guest has a request to remember on every visit (high floor, extra pillow, allergy):

1. Press **Add standing note** on the card.
2. Type the **Note to remember on every visit**.
3. **Level**: **Info** (a yellow box in the panel and the calendar) or **Important** (reading it must be confirmed before a reservation is saved).
4. Save.

## Merging duplicate cards {#merge-guests}

If the same person was written twice, the card shows a **Possible duplicate** warning.

1. Press **Compare and merge**.
2. Choose the card to keep with **Keep this card**.
3. Press **Merge**.

The other card's reservations, payments, notes and company link move to the card that stays. The old card is not deleted; it is marked "merged". A merge cannot be undone; if you are not sure, do not do it.

## Companies and billing details {#companies}

Enter the company details of guests who want an invoice once, and copy them with one click every time. Bismuth does not issue invoices; you paste the details into your invoicing program.

1. Open the **Companies** tab on the **Guests** screen or **Companies** in the menu.
2. Press **New company**: **Company name**, **Address**, **City**, **Tax office**, **Tax number**, **Phone**, **Email**, **IBAN**, **Contact person**.
3. Make companies you use often a **Favourite company**; they stay at the top of the list.
4. To link a guest to a company, press **Link a company** on the guest card's **Company** tab and pick the company.

To copy, press the company's copy button. **Copy format**: **One per line** or **Single line**. A company's **History and guests** shows the linked guests and their stays.

## Blacklist {#blacklist}

Unwanted guests are put on the blacklist; they are caught by name or phone when a reservation is written.

To check someone: open **Guests → Blacklist** and type a full name or phone in the **Check** box. It is compared as you type; you see "No blacklist match." or the matching person.

![Blacklist](blacklist)

To add someone:

1. Press **Add to blacklist**.
2. **Full name** and **Reason** are required; add the **Phone** and ID number if you have them.
3. **Severity**: **Warn** (a red warning appears on the reservation and you go on after confirming) or **Block** (only the owner or manager can save the reservation).
4. Choose the **Reason type** and **Date of the incident** and save.

You can also add someone from the guest card; the name, phone and ID come from the card.

To take someone off the list, press **Remove** on the line, type the **Reason for removal** (for example "paid the debt") and choose **Remove from the list**. The record is not deleted; it stays under the **Removed** tab with who removed it and why.
