# Month-end check and reports

When the month ends, the hotel owner checks a few things: card and IBAN totals, salaries, revenue and profit, where most money went, and the ministry report. The **Month-end check** screen brings all of these together; the **Reports** screen gives charts and comparisons period by period. All the figures are worked out from the reservations, payments and expenses you entered; nothing is typed twice.

## Month-end check {#month-end-check}

Open **Money → Month-end check** in the left menu. Choose the month in the box at the top, or use the arrows to go to the previous or next month. At the top right are **Export to Excel** and **Print / PDF**.

At the very top, the **Revenue**, **Expenses**, **Profit** and **Occupancy** boxes show the change from the previous month.

The **Checklist** has five steps:

1. **Card and IBAN totals**: the amount waiting to be checked at the bank. Compare card and IBAN lines with the bank statement and mark them received (x) in the ledger. See [Receiving the money](guide:payments#handover).
2. **Salaries**: the amount still to pay. See [Staff and salaries](guide:staff#salary-table).
3. **Revenue, expenses and profit**: the month's profit.
4. **Where the money went**: the biggest expense item.
5. **Ministry of Tourism report**: the last day and the days left; **Open** takes you to the Tourism statistics screen. See [Ministry statistics](guide:tourism).

Steps 1, 2 and 5 are ticked from the records by themselves (green). You tick steps 3 and 4 yourself after looking at them; these ticks are saved to your account and look the same on every computer. Other users do not see your ticks.

The boxes below:

- **Payment methods**: each method's **Total**, **Received (x)** and **Pending**; under it, money that came to the IBAN by channel (to compare with the bank statement). Cash is shown for information only.
- **Tourism statistics**: the month's reporting dates; **Prepare the table** takes you to the ministry table.
- **Top spending**: the biggest items by staff, product groups and expense descriptions. If the same expense was written in different ways, merge them with the **Description list** in the ledger.
- **Salaries**: for each employee the salary, advances, days off, unused day-off pay, net due, paid and remaining.
- **Rate sources**: which rate fixed the base-currency value of the records in other currencies (see below).

The download button in the corner of each table saves only that table as a file.

## Reports {#reports}

Open **Reports → Reports** in the left menu.

- **Period**: **This month**, **Last month**, **This year** or **Date range**. **Compare with**: **Previous period**, **Same period last year** or **No comparison**. The chosen dates are written under the boxes.
- Tabs: **Summary**, **Yearly summary**, **Occupancy and indicators**, **Cash and payments**, **Expenses**, **Staff**.
- **Summary**: the **Revenue**, **Profit**, **Expenses** and **Occupancy** boxes; the **Daily revenue** chart (press a day to open that day's breakdown), **Income details**, **Sales channels**, **Expense sections**, **Top spending**, **Supply costs** and **Rate sources**.
- **Occupancy and indicators**: occupancy by month, by day of the week, by room type and by room; **Average nightly rate** (room revenue ÷ nights sold), **Revenue per room** (room revenue ÷ sellable nights), **Average stay** and **Returning guests**.
- **Cash and payments**: amounts by payment method, money not yet received, non-cash lines waiting to be checked and the handover history.
- **Expenses** and **Staff**: expenses by month, how expenses are spread and staff costs.
- Charts have a **Chart** and a **Table** view; in the table you read the figures line by line.
- **Export to Excel** saves the whole report as one Excel file; each table's download button gives only that table as Excel or CSV. **Print / PDF** prints the report on paper or to a PDF.
- The **Supply costs** box shows the period's purchases with products by product category; purchases are entered on the **Supplies** screen.
- The **Rate sources** box says which rate fixed the value of records in other currencies: **Market mid rate** (with the source's name), **TCMB rate**, **Hotel rate**, **Rate entered by hand** or **No rate (not in the totals)**. Records without a rate are left out of the totals; they are added once that day's rate is entered on the **Exchange rates** screen.

Days closed for sale are taken out of the sellable nights when occupancy is worked out.

Only people with the right see the money reports (revenue, profit, expenses). The reception role sees occupancy and room-night figures but not the money figures. The **Owner's expenses** section is seen only by those allowed to see it.
