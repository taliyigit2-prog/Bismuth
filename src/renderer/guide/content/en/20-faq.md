# Frequently asked questions and troubleshooting

The most common questions and what to do. If you have a problem whose answer is not here, tell the hotel owner.

## The internet is down; can I work? {#offline}

Yes. Bismuth works without the internet; reservations, payments, expenses and KBS marks are all written as usual. Only the exchange rates, the weather and the holiday check are not updated: foreign-currency payments use the last known rate. Everything refreshes by itself when the internet comes back.

## I wrote a record wrongly; how do I delete it? {#undo-mistake}

In Bismuth records are not deleted; they are corrected or cancelled. That way it is always clear who changed what.

- A reservation: correct it on the **Nights** tab of the panel or use **More actions → Cancel reservation**.
- A payment or expense: on the **Ledger and cash** screen, **Edit** or **Void** in the line's actions.
- A task: **More actions → Cancel task**.

## It says the room is taken but it looks free {#room-busy}

Check the days you chose for that room in the calendar: there may be the last night of another reservation or a day closed for sale (striped). The check-out day is not a night; a new guest can check in to a room on the day the previous guest leaves. Reservations in the park area do not hold a room.

## Why did the bar's colour not change? {#color-not-changed}

The colour follows payments. If you recorded the payment as an "Extra sale" rather than on the reservation, the colour does not change. Open the reservation's **Money** tab and check the payment is there. A payment is spread over the nights in order; if the amount covers one night, only the first night shows as paid.

## It says "You do not have permission for this" {#no-permission}

Your role does not allow this work. Ask the hotel owner if needed; the owner can change your role's permissions in **Settings → Users and roles**.

## I forgot my password or PIN {#forgot}

On the sign-in screen press **Forgot password** and set a new password with one of your recovery codes. If you have no code, the hotel owner gives you a temporary password with **Reset password**. If you forgot your PIN, sign in with your password and choose **Change PIN** in the account menu. See [Forgot your password](guide:getting-started#forgot-password).

## The screen locks by itself {#auto-lock}

After a while without activity, the window locks for safety. Type your PIN and carry on. If the time needs changing, the hotel owner sets it in **Settings → General → Screen lock**.

## The cash in the drawer does not match "Should be in the drawer" {#cash-mismatch}

1. In today's ledger, check the cash payments and the expenses marked **Paid from the drawer**; is a payment or expense missing?
2. Were advances from the drawer recorded on the staff screen?
3. Is there a line recorded in the wrong currency?

If you cannot find the difference, write it in the **Day note** and tell the owner.

## The text on the screen is too small or too crowded {#zoom}

The program menu has **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** under **View** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>-</kbd> on Windows, <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>-</kbd> on a Mac); **Actual size** brings it back. If lines feel crowded, set **Settings → General → Density** to **Comfortable**.

## The program does not open or says "Your data could not be opened" {#cannot-open}

Close the program and open it again. If the problem stays, tell the hotel owner and **do not delete** the data folder shown on the screen. Your data is not deleted; if needed it is restored from the last backup. See [Backup and moving data](guide:backup).

## About the program {#about}

The **System → About** screen shows the program's version, the data folder, the result of the data check and the open source components used. If someone helping you asks for the version, look here.

Bismuth contains no tracking, no ads and no automatic updates. Your data stays in the data folder on this computer and is not sent anywhere. From the internet it only reads the market exchange rate, the weather for the hotel's city and, once a year, holiday dates; the **Update online** switch on the Exchange rates screen turns all of it off.
