# Shift handover and notes

When shifts change, the person leaving tells the person arriving what is waiting. Bismuth prepares most of this by itself: arrivals not in yet, who is in the house tonight, KBS gaps, unpaid balances, open tasks and issues, cash in the drawer. You only write what you want to add.

Open **Daily work → Shift handover and notes** in the left menu. The screen has three tabs: **Handover**, **My notes**, **Board**.

![Shift handover](shift)

## Writing the handover (end of shift) {#write-handover}

1. On the **Handover** tab, look at the **Automatic summary**: **Arrivals not in yet**, **In the house tonight**, **KBS gaps**, **Unpaid balances**, **Open tasks and issues**, **Cash in the drawer**, **Check-out time passed, not checked out**. These are calculated from the records; nobody types them.
2. In the **Handover note** box under **Write the handover**, write what you want to add to the summary. For example: "503 arrives late, leave the door open", "102's air-conditioning remote is at reception".
3. Press **Save handover**.

The handover is kept together with the summary at that moment; figures that change later do not alter an old handover.

> [!tip]
> If you have nothing special to say, leave the note empty and still press **Save handover**; the next person reads the automatic summary.

## Reading the handover (start of shift) {#read-handover}

If there is an unread handover, the Today screen shows an **Unread shift handover** warning.

1. Press **Read the handover** or open the **Shift handover and notes** screen.
2. In the **Unread handover** box, read the note and the **Summary at handover**.
3. Press **I've read it**.

Who wrote it, and who read it when, stays on record. You cannot mark your own handover as read. Handovers of the last 14 days are under **Past handovers**.

Shift times are set in **Settings → Shifts** (for example Day 09:00–21:00, Night 21:00–09:00).

## Notes to self {#personal-notes}

The **My notes** tab is only for you; nobody else can see it.

1. Press **Add a note to self** and write the note.
2. If you like, switch on **Remind me** and choose a day and **Time**. When the time comes, the note is highlighted.
3. When it is done, **Close note**.

## Board {#board}

The **Board** tab is a notice board everyone on the staff sees (for example "Water cut on Saturday").

1. Press **Post on the board** and write the note.
2. If it is important, tick **Pin to the top**; pinned notes stay at the top.
3. When the note is out of date, **Remove from board**.
