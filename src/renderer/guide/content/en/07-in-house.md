# In-house guests and KBS

Every person staying in the hotel must by law be reported to the Identity Notification System (**KBS**). Bismuth does not connect to KBS; you enter people in KBS as usual. Bismuth shows you **who has been reported and who is missing**, and helps the night shift recognise everyone.

## Opening the screen {#open-in-house}

Open **Daily work → In-house guests** in the left menu. The screen shows who is staying now, room by room: people, identifying notes, KBS status, check-out date and balance.

![In-house guests](in-house)

The boxes at the top: **Occupied rooms**, **People in the house**, **Leaving today**, **KBS gaps**.

- Type a name, room or identifying note in the search box (for example "glasses").
- Switch on **Only KBS gaps** to keep only the people not yet reported.

## The "Reported to KBS" mark {#kbs-mark}

1. Enter the person in KBS (as usual).
2. In Bismuth press the **Reported to KBS** button on that person's line.
3. The line shows "Reported to KBS" with the time and your name.

If you marked it by mistake, press **Undo the mark**. A person not reported is shown in red; a **KBS missing** warning also appears on the Today screen and on the bar in the calendar.

> [!warning]
> If nobody is recorded in a room, it says "Nobody is recorded in this room". Add the people staying and report them to KBS; an empty room counts as a KBS gap.

## Someone who arrives later {#add-person}

If someone joins a room later:

1. Press **Add person** (or the room's own add button).
2. Choose the **Room** and type the **Full name**.
3. **Identifying note (optional)**: a short description so the night shift recognises them without asking ("red hair, glasses").
4. If you have entered them in KBS, tick the KBS box and save.

Bismuth never asks for or keeps ID numbers.

## KBS check-out at departure {#kbs-checkout}

When a guest leaves, do their KBS check-out too. When you press **Check out** on the reservation, you mark the people whose KBS check-out is done in the window that opens. Those not marked stay in the **Awaiting KBS check-out** list at the bottom of this screen: do the check-out in KBS, then press **KBS check-out done** here.

If someone left before the reservation ended, choose **Person actions → Mark as left** on the **Occupants** tab of the reservation panel.

## Night list {#night-list}

The **Print** button prints an A4 list for the night shift: room, people with their identifying notes, KBS status, check-in and check-out, and note. Even if the screen is in the dark theme, the list prints black on white paper.
