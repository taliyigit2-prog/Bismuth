# Room status and issues

The **Room status** screen shows at a glance each room's cleaning status, who is staying, today's check-ins and check-outs, issues and whether the room is closed for sale. The **Issues** screen records problems in rooms and common areas, with photos.

## Room statuses {#room-states}

Each room has a status:

- **Clean**: the room is ready and can be sold.
- **Dirty**: it needs cleaning. When a guest checks out, the room becomes dirty **by itself**.
- **Inspected**: the cleaning has been checked (an optional step).
- **Maintenance**: work is being done in the room; the room is closed for sale in the calendar.
- **Out of order**: the room cannot be used; it is closed for sale in the calendar.

## Floor plan {#floor-plan}

Open **Daily work → Room status** in the left menu. Rooms appear as boxes, floor by floor.

![Room status, floor plan](rooms)

- The box's colour and text tell the status; inside you see the guest staying, **Arriving today**, **Departing today**, the number of open issues and **Closed for sale**.
- Use the filter at the top to see only the rooms in one status (for example only dirty ones).
- The **Floor plan** and **List** buttons switch between the two views. The list also shows who made the last change and the deep clean counter.

## Marking a room clean {#mark-clean}

When the housekeeper has cleaned a room (or writes "302+" on WhatsApp):

1. Press the **Cleaned** button in the room's box.
2. "Room 302 marked as “Clean”" appears.

To choose another status, click the room; in the window that opens choose the status under **Change status**. You can write a short **Note** (for example "sheet missing"); the note is cleared when the status changes.

> [!tip]
> A room set to **Maintenance** or **Out of order** is closed for sale in the calendar by itself; choosing another status puts it back on sale. If the room is occupied tonight it cannot be closed; choose the status again after the guest leaves.

## Reporting an issue {#issues}

When a problem is seen in a room or common area (broken toilet seat, dripping tap, lamp not working):

1. Open **Operations → Issues** in the left menu and press **Report an issue**.
2. **Where?**: choose **Room** and pick the room, or choose **Common area** and type the place (lobby, roof, stairs).
3. Type a **Short title** (for example "Toilet seat broken") and, if you like, **Details**.
4. **Severity**: **Low**, **Medium** or **Urgent**.
5. If the room cannot be used, tick **Close the room for sale**; until the issue is fixed the room shows as closed in the calendar.
6. Add **Photos** (you can take them with the phone and add them).
7. Save.

![Report an issue window](issue-dialog)

Reception and the owner see the issue at once: on the Today screen, as a wrench icon next to the room number in the calendar, and on the floor plan.

## Planning and closing an issue {#resolve-issue}

Click the issue on the **Issues** screen:

- **Plan the repair**: type **Which day?** and **Who will do it?** (for example "plumber") and press **Save the plan**. The issue becomes **Planned**.
- When the repair is done, write **What was done** under **Close as fixed** (for example "seal replaced") and press **Mark as fixed**. If the room was closed for sale, it opens again.
- If there was a cost, record it in the ledger as an expense; the issue itself keeps no amount.

Photos of fixed issues are deleted by themselves after a while (the issue text stays); to delete them now press **Delete photos now**.

Use the filter at the top to see **Open**, **Planned** and **Fixed** issues separately.
